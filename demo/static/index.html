These are the (fine-tuned) cache keys most similar to your image — the exp(−β(1 − cos)) affinity weights their one-hot labels into the final logits.
How it works: final logits = 100·q·Tᵀ (zero-shot CLIP) + α · exp(−β(1 − q·Kᵀ))·V (cache). K = 160 support image features (16/class), V = one-hot labels. In Tip-Adapter-F the keys K were fine-tuned with AdamW (cross-entropy, validation-selected checkpoint); in Tip-Adapter they stay frozen. Recorded test accuracy at 16-shot: zero-shot 85.25 · Tip-Adapter 88.83 · Tip-Adapter-F 90.12 (mean over 3 seeds).
